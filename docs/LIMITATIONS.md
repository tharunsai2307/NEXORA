# NEXORA — System Boundaries, Known Limitations & Future Roadmap

**Product:** NEXORA — Edge-AI-Based Smart Building Safety and Occupancy Digital Twin System  
**Classification:** Academic Engineering Capstone & Advanced Research Prototype  
**Life-Safety Disclaimer:** NOT A CERTIFIED PHYSICAL LIFE-SAFETY OR EMERGENCY DISPATCH SYSTEM

---

## 1. Explicit Academic & Simulation Boundaries

In adherence to engineering ethics and academic integrity, the following boundaries of the NEXORA implementation are explicitly identified:

### 1.1 Simulated Virtual IoT Fleet
- **Simulation Notice:** All sensor readings (Temperature, Relative Humidity, Smoke Ionization Concentration, Air Quality Index, and Room Occupancy) are generated in software by the Java `SimulationEngineServiceImpl` and mathematical drift equations.
- **Hardware Disconnection:** There are **NO** physical IoT sensors, Zigbee gateways, LoRaWAN transceivers, or BACnet/Modbus physical interfaces attached to this software implementation.

### 1.2 Synthetic AI Training & Calibration Data
- **Synthetic Benchmark Notice:** The Isolation Forest anomaly detector and Random Forest occupancy forecaster were trained, calibrated, and evaluated on synthetic numerical datasets generated by `app/training/synthetic_data.py`.
- **Domain Baseline:** Features were modeled after published ASHRAE thermal comfort standards (Standard 55) and EPA Air Quality Index guidelines, but have not been validated against multi-year physical building empirical telemetry.

### 1.3 Simulated Evacuation Clearance
- **Drill Context:** Room occupant clearance during active evacuations is calculated algorithmically to demonstrate dynamic room-clearing progress bars, Dijkstra waypoint rerouting, and post-mortem metrics.
- **No Physical Dispatch:** The platform does not trigger municipal 911 dispatch, municipal fire department consoles, or physical building horn-strobes/sprinklers.

---

## 2. Technical & Algorithmic Limitations

1. **Crowd Dynamics & Bottleneck Physics:**
   - The dynamic evacuation engine utilizes shortest-path graph optimization (weighted Dijkstra).
   - It does not account for continuous micro-agent behavioral physics (e.g. panic behavior, bottleneck queuing around narrow doorways, or smoke inhalation incapacitation models).
2. **Simplified Structural Topologies:**
   - Building geometry is currently modeled using box cuboid coordinates `(posX, posY, posZ, width, depth, height)`.
   - Complex non-Euclidean architectural elements (e.g., spiral staircases, curved atrium walls, complex HVAC ducts) are simplified into bounding rectangular volumes.
3. **Single Building Evacuation Concurrency:**
   - While the building fleet explorer manages multiple buildings, active real-time evacuation simulations are currently optimized for single building drill sessions.
4. **Airflow & Smoke Plume Physics:**
   - Environmental spread across connected rooms during fire scenarios is simulated via adjacency distance propagation rather than continuous Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD).

## 3. Future Enhancements & Commercial Roadmap

If transitioned to commercial deployment, the following enhancements are planned:

- [ ] **BACnet & Modbus IP Connectors:** Ingest live hardware telemetry from physical Johnson Controls, Siemens Desigo, or Schneider Electric building automation systems.
- [ ] **BIM / IFC CAD Ingestion:** Automated parser to convert Industry Foundation Classes (IFC) and Autodesk Revit files directly into Three.js 3D meshes and evacuation navigation graphs.
- [ ] **Microscopic Agent Crowd Simulation:** Integration with OpenABM or custom WebGPU compute shaders to model individual occupant panic and door congestion dynamics.
- [ ] **Edge TPU & NPU Acceleration:** Deploy quantization-optimized models (ONNX / TensorFlow Lite) onto embedded Coral Edge TPUs or NVIDIA Jetson gateway hardware located on each floor.
- [ ] **Mobile AR Egress Navigator:** Augmented Reality (AR) mobile application guiding building occupants to the nearest safe exit using phone cameras and WebXR.
